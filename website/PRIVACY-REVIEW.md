The bilingual draft is at `/it/privacy/` and `/en/privacy/`. It remains incomplete and is not approved as a final Article 13 notice. This review changes copy and presentation only; it does not change enquiry handling, hosting or tracking.

- The owner confirmed on 8 October 2026 that Avv. Marco Rodeghiero personally is the data controller. Existing approved name, address, telephone, email and PEC are reused.
- Source inspection confirms a static site without contact/booking forms, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, embedded maps or social widgets. The only browser script controls the mobile menu. Website code does not write cookies, local storage or session storage. Fonts and photographs are served by the website.
- A fresh, unsigned-in browser visited all ten currently published bilingual main pages on 8 October 2026: requests went only to giobertox.github.io; no cookies or browser storage entries were observed, and there were no forms, iframes or external scripts. This is a scoped observation of this version, not a guarantee about every possible browser/provider condition.
- GitHub Pages hosts the published preview. GitHub explicitly documents security logging of visitor IP addresses. Absence of website analytics does not imply absence of hosting data.
- The approved public email address uses Gmail. This does not establish the account's contractual arrangements, provider role, internal access controls, storage location or retention.
- Requests use phone/email and appointments are confirmed directly by the office, as recorded in the project requirements. No client correspondence or accounts were accessed.

## Editorial decisions and unresolved facts

- Used the reference site's separation of controller, data/purposes, recipients, retention and rights as structural inspiration. No wording was copied.
- Removed the proposed Article 6(1)(b) basis as an assertion about actual processing. The actual purposes and bases require confirmation for each activity, especially enquiries containing third-party, special-category or criminal-offence data.
- Do not claim no international transfers: GitHub's own notice describes international processing. Actual provider roles, agreements, locations and applicable safeguards remain TBC.
- Do not invent retention periods or promise deletion, authorised-only access, a particular security measure, a processor agreement or a complete recipient list.
- DPO appointment (if applicable), access to enquiries, actual providers/recipients, enquiry processing, retention and any solely automated significant decisions outside the website remain unconfirmed.
- Rights wording is conditional. Portability is tied to automated processing based on consent/contract; consent withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing. Response timing and complaint guidance describe statutory rules, not evidence of an internal workflow.
- The public draft visibly identifies unresolved details. Completion and factual/legal approval are still needed before it is used as the definitive notice.
